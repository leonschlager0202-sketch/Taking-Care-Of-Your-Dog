Grooming and Hygiene

• Coat Maintenance: Brushing removes dead hair, prevents painful matting, and distributes natural skin oils. The frequency depends heavily on their coat type (e.g., daily for long coats, weekly for short coats). • Nail Care: Overgrown nails alter a dog's gait, pushing their toes upward and causing chronic joint pain or arthritis. As a rule of thumb, if you hear them clicking on the floor, they need a trim.
• Dental Hygiene: Periodontal disease can lead to bacteria entering the bloodstream, damaging the heart, kidneys, and liver. Ideally, their teeth should be brushed daily with dog-safe toothpaste. • Ear & Eye Inspections: Checking ears for redness, odor, or debris prevents severe yeast and bacterial infections. Keeping eyes clear of discharge prevents tear-duct irritation.
