Training your dog

Positive Reinforcement (+R): You add something the dog wants (like a treat or praise) to make a behavior happen more often (e.g., giving a piece of chicken when the dog sits on command) Negative Reinforcement (-R): You remove something aversive or uncomfortable (like easing leash pressure) when the dog performs the correct behavior, making the behavior happen more often (e.g., releasing tension on the leash as soon as the dog steps towards you)
Positive Punishment (+P): You add something unpleasant or aversive (like a sharp verbal "no" or leash pop) to make a behavior happen less often Negative Punishment (-N): You remove something the dog values (like taking away attention or turning your back) to make a behavior happen less often (e.g., stopping a game when they bite too hard)
